Customizable select is coming to Safari 27, and this article explains that developers must always provide text content or accessible text attributes for their option elements. It demonstrates improved UX, accessibility compliance, and progressive enhancement by comparing icon-only versus labeled options, emphasizing that visual enhancements should supplement rather than replace textual content.
